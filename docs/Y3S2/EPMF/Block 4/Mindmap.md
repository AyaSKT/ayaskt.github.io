## Lecture 14

```mindmap

# 公司形态 Company Forms

## 工程师相关性

- 权力，责任和决策链
- 注册，融资，股权分配
- 董事法律责任
- IP，供应链，雇员责任最终落到公司结构上

# 有限公司 Limited Company

## 性质

- 独立法律人格 Separate Legal Personality
- 可拥有财产，雇佣员工，借款，贷款
- 可起诉或被起诉
- 公司财务与个人财务分开
- 利润先缴 Corporation Tax

## 角色

- Members / Shareholders > 所有者
- Directors > 管理者
- 股东和董事可重合，但法律身份不同

## Limited by Shares

- 股东责任通常限于未支付股份金额
- 适合普通商业公司
- 便于融资，雇人，长期经营
- 风险与个人资产相对分离

## Company Limited by Guarantee

- 通常没有股东
- 成员承诺固定担保金额
- 常见于 social enterprises, clubs, charities

## Public Limited Company (PLC)

- 股份可在公开市场交易
- 更容易公众融资
- 监管，披露，治理要求更高

# 个体经营 Sole Trader

## 性质

- 个人以自己身份经营业务
- 成立最简单
- 利润纳税后归个人
- 亏损和债务由个人承担
- 可雇佣员工
- 适合小规模，低风险，低融资需求业务

# 合伙企业 Partnership

## Ordinary Partnership

- 两个或多个合伙人共同经营
- 分享利润
- 共同承担损失和业务账单
- 每个合伙人对自己利润份额纳税

## 风险控制形态

- Limited Partnership
- Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- 公司也可以作为 legal person 成为 partner

# 所有权 Ownership

## 股份口径

- 所有权通过 shares 表示
- Shares 越多，ownership percentage 越高
- $\text{Ownership Percentage} = \frac{\text{Shares Owned}}{\text{Total Shares}}$

## 权益价值

- $\text{Equity Value} = \text{Ownership Percentage} \times \text{Company Valuation}$

# 公司估值 Company Valuation

## 性质

- 市场，投资者或买方认为公司值多少钱
- 初创早期可近似 founders' investment
- 运营后不只看现金

## 价值来源

- IP
- Products
- Assets
- Services
- Customer Base
- Revenue / Trading Performance

## 敏感时点

- Attracting Investment
- Trade Sale
- IPO

# 股份价格与稀释 Share Price and Dilution

## 方法

- $\text{New Shares} = \frac{\text{New Investment}}{\text{Share Price}}$
- $\text{Total Shares}_{new} = \text{Old Shares} + \text{New Shares}$
- $\text{Ownership}_{old} = \frac{\text{Old Shares}}{\text{Total Shares}_{new}}$

## 性质

- 新投资通常稀释老股东
- 按低股价融资稀释更大
- 按新估值提高股价可降低稀释幅度

# 市值 Market Capitalisation

## 公式

- $\text{Market Capitalisation} = \text{Number of Shares} \times \text{Share Price}$

## 区分

- Brand Value > 品牌本身商业价值
- Company Valuation > 公司整体价值
- Market Capitalisation > 上市公司公开市场价值

## 性质

- 市值受市场情绪影响
- 不是固定物理量
- 是投资者对未来价值的集体判断

```


## Lecture 15

```mindmap

# 公司成长 Company Growth

## 结构变化原因

- 人数增加，直接监督变难
- 产品，客户，地区增加
- 决策需要分层
- 权力开始下放
- 流程，预算，汇报，绩效指标出现

## Command and Control

- 小组织里有效
- 规模扩大后容易形成瓶颈
- 需要转向更合适的结构

# Greiner 成长模型

## 性质

- 组织随规模扩大经历成长阶段
- 每个阶段靠一种管理方式成长
- 每个阶段也会制造下一种危机
- 关键词：Evolution and Revolution

## 阶段与危机

- Creativity > Leadership Crisis
- Direction > Autonomy Crisis
- Delegation > Control Crisis
- Coordination > Red-Tape Crisis
- Collaboration > Stress / Burnout

# 创业型组织 Entrepreneurial Organisation

## 性质

- 标准化 Standardisation 低
- 正式化 Formalisation 低
- 管理层级少
- 决策集中于 founder / entrepreneur
- 管理幅度 Span of Control 宽
- 响应环境变化快

## 适用边界

- 适合早期探索
- 不适合无限增长
- 人数，产品，客户增加后需要结构化

# Miles and Snow 战略类型

## Defender

- 效率 Efficiency
- 稳定 Stability
- 聚焦明确市场
- 结构 formal / centralised / controlled

## Prospector

- 创新 Innovation
- 变化 Change
- 持续寻找新市场
- 结构 flexible / informal / decentralised

## Analyzer

- 平衡风险与利润
- 保留核心业务
- 谨慎尝试新机会
- 常用 mixed / matrix structure

## Reactor

- No clear focus
- 被环境推动
- Strategy 与 structure 匹配差

# 组织结构 Organisation Structures

## Divisional Structure

- 按 product / customer segment / geography 划分
- 每个 division 具备相对自治
- 优势：Flexibility, Accountability, GM development
- 风险：Duplication, Rivalry, Loss of Economies of Scale, Strategy Alignment

## Flat Structure

- 中间管理层少
- 优势：Lower operating costs, Improved communication, Motivation
- 风险：Large organisation difficulty, Low retention, Power struggles
- 适合 early startup, innovation, high communication density

## Matrix Structure

- 多重汇报线
- 同时连接 functional manager 与 project manager
- 目标：functional expertise + project responsiveness
- 优势：Collaboration, Efficiency, Skill development
- 风险：Unclear roles, Slow decisions, Overload, Hard performance measurement

## Network Structure

- 多个组织共同完成产品或服务
- 保留核心能力，外部伙伴承担其他活动
- 优势：Focus, Lower fixed cost, Flexible, Specialised expertise
- 风险：Reliability, Consistency, Secrecy, IP leakage, Weak control

## Team-Based Structure

- 自管理或半自治团队作为基本单元
- Horizontal work
- Decentralized authority
- Cross-functional teams
- Department boundaries weakened

# 结构选择 Structure Choice

## 匹配逻辑

- Early startup > entrepreneurial / flat
- Rapid product expansion > functional + project coordination
- 多产品 / 多地区 > divisional
- 工程项目密集 > matrix
- 全球供应链依赖 > network
- 创新和跨职能协作 > team-based

## 核心原则

- Structure serves strategy
- Innovation strategy > speed, experiments, cross-functional collaboration
- Efficiency strategy > standardisation, cost control, stable execution

```


## Lecture 16

```mindmap

# 公司战略 Company Strategy

## 性质

- 长期方向和范围
- 通过资源配置获得优势
- 满足市场需求和利益相关者期望
- 一组选择：where to play and how to win
- $\text{Strategy} = \text{Long-term Advantage} + \text{Resource Alignment}$

## 需要的信息

- Internal Knowledge > strengths, limitations, resources, capabilities
- External Knowledge > competitors, markets, customers, regulation, technology change

# 战略层级 Strategy Inside Companies

## 局部战略

- Product Strategy
- Business Unit Strategy
- Technology Strategy
- Manufacturing Strategy
- Sales and Marketing Strategy

## 对齐原则

- 有效公司通常只有一个 overall strategy
- 局部战略是整体战略的展开
- 产品，制造，技术，销售不能互相拉扯

# 三个战略问题

## Customer

- 目标客户是谁
- 市场或细分市场在哪里
- 支付能力和使用场景是什么

## Offering

- 提供什么独特价值
- 解决什么问题
- 节省时间，节省成本，或创造新能力

## Capabilities

- 需要哪些能力稳定交付 value proposition
- Suppliers
- Skills
- Infrastructure
- Money
- Technology

# 技术战略 Technology Strategy

## 核心选择

- Technology Leader vs Technology Follower
- First-mover advantage 是否能被客户付费
- 哪些技术必须拥有 IP
- 哪些技术可以 licence / buy
- 技术是否真是核心竞争点

## 理想形态

- 关键 IP 让产品难以复制
- 非关键技术通过购买或授权解决

# 战略所有权 Strategy Ownership

## 典型 Owner

- Overall business strategy > CEO and senior leadership team
- Marketing strategy > Marketing Director
- Technology strategy > CTO
- Operations strategy > Operations Manager / COO
- Quality strategy > Quality leader

## 性质

- Leadership owns strategy
- Execution distributed across organization
- 工程师通过技术选择，质量，成本，交付影响战略执行

# Strategy vs Tactics

## Strategy

- Long term
- Where to play, how to win
- Leadership owned
- 方向和路线图

## Tactics

- Short / medium term
- What action to take
- Department / team executed
- 执行层动作

# 战略工具与指标

## Business Strategy Building Blocks

- Vision
- Objectives
- Business and marketplace analysis
- Competitive advantage / USP
- Execution framework and KPI

## SWOT Analysis

- Strengths > internal
- Weaknesses > internal
- Opportunities > external
- Threats > external
- 用现实条件约束战略选择

## KPI

- 用可测指标追踪战略执行
- 按部门定义
- 共同贡献到公司整体目标

## 财务 KPIs

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Net Profit
- Operating Profit
- EBITDA
- Free Cash Flow

## 部门 KPIs

- Manufacturing > yield, cost, throughput
- Sales > revenue, market share
- Engineering > defect rate, delivery milestone, technical risk closure

# 工程职业路线 Engineering Careers

## Industry Engineer

- System Architect / System Engineer
- Design Engineer
- Test Development Engineer
- Manufacturing / Production Engineer
- Equipment / Plant Engineer
- Quality Engineer
- Sales Engineer
- Applications Engineer

## Academic Engineer

- Research limitations
- High-quality research papers
- Breakthrough discoveries
- 竞争激烈

## Outside Engineering

- Finance
- Law
- Journalism
- Marketing
- Sales
- Property Development
- Writing
- Entrepreneurship

## 可迁移能力 Transferable Skills

- Define problems
- Analyse problems
- Numerical literacy
- Computer literacy
- Pure logical thought
- Innovative solutions
- Teamwork and communication

## 个人职业战略

- 目标客户是谁
- 提供什么独特价值
- 需要哪些能力持续交付

```
